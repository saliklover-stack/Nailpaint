If anything is insufficient:

If everything is available:

Allow confirmation.

After confirmation:

2. Add finished goods.

4. Calculate production cost.
5. Save production transaction.

All operations must occur in ONE database transaction.

If any step fails, roll everything back.

11. PRODUCTION COST

Calculate product cost from consumed material costs.

Initially use weighted average cost.

Example:

Chemical = SAR 500
Brush = SAR 100
Glass = SAR 300
Cap = SAR 150
Pigment = SAR 200

Total = SAR 1,250

Production quantity = 100

Unit cost = SAR 12.50

Store production cost.

Do not hard-code costs.

==================================================
12. SALES

Sales entry:

Customer
Product
Quantity
Selling price
Payment method
Date

Selling price should automatically load from product settings.

Authorized users can override it.

When sale is confirmed:

1. Check finished stock.
2. Prevent negative stock.
3. Deduct finished stock.
4. Calculate revenue.
5. Calculate COGS.
6. Calculate gross profit.
7. Create payment record if paid.
8. Create customer receivable if credit.
9. Create stock ledger record.

Formula:

Revenue = Quantity × Selling Price

COGS = Quantity × Product Cost

Gross Profit = Revenue − COGS
